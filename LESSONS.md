Future candidate mechanisms should target **observable semantic result mismatches**, define output shape, question grain and calibrated validations, and screen reference-label quality; not blindly extend prompt, retry, or LoRA steps.


- [Verified: source-value original and repeat Actions 43921074189, 43924579052] An exact, physically grounded value-to-column hint improved same-source Qwen3.5-9B strict BIRD TRAIN16 EX from **9→11** on first run and **8→11** on same-cohort rerun, both without regressions. - [Verified: independent BIRD TRAIN source-value replication Actions 43926576493, 43927133612] **Real database evidence can be misleading when the entity role is wrong.** A prior 16-case, 4-DB sample gave physical SQLite value-column hints an apparent +2/+3 strict EX, but a second pre-frozen 16-case population from 4 different DBs produced **9→5/16, zero rescues and four regressions**. Source-only literal matching recovered `Apricot` and `Yogurt` as ingredients when the question mentioned a **recipe title**, and `White` as a surname when the question asked **race**; extra hints also distorted ratio denominator and units. Thus raw values are not semantic evidence unless bound to question role, phrase span and type. Do not chase the first winning cohort or incrementally tune this method on the now-exposed 32 cases. Treat independent-source replication as the veto gate, with actual EX and paired regressions (not tool execution) the objective.

- [Verified: physical pruned-description paired BIRD DEV12 #43828964676, aggregate #133177701242] Removing publisher column-description noise is not equivalent to discovering semantic information. Reject this particular pruning intervention and keep #69 closed/unmerged. Do not chase improved bag EX as if it were end-to-end strict semantic correctness.

- [Verified: source-disjoint Qwen3.5-9B BIRD TRAIN role8 #44012902052] Eliminating obvious question-role mismatches in physical literal lookup may help avoid false hints but did NOT improve genuine SQL semantics: original vs role-filtered strict EX **6→6/8**, direct EX **6→5/8** (0 rescues/0 regressions under Guarded), extra runtime +28%. No further tuning on these exposed 8 questions; prioritise question intent, relational grain, aggregation denominator and independently adjudicated publisher-label quality before more training.
